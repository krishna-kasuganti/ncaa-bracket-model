NCAA BRACKET MODEL — DATA DICTIONARY
=====================================

FILE: MTeams.csv
PURPOSE: Master lookup table that maps numeric TeamIDs to team names.
COLUMNS: TeamID, TeamName, FirstD1Season, LastD1Season
NOTES: TeamID never changes from year to year. Used to translate IDs to
       readable team names when joining with other files.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

FILE: MSeasons.csv
PURPOSE: Maps each season to its calendar year and date information.
COLUMNS: Season, DayZero, RegionW, RegionX, RegionY, RegionZ
NOTES: Season refers to the year the tournament was played in, not the
       year the season started. DayZero is used to convert DayNum values
       into real calendar dates.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

FILE: MNCAATourneySeeds.csv
PURPOSE: Records the seed assigned to every team in every tournament.
COLUMNS: Season, Seed, TeamID
NOTES: Between 64 to 68 rows per year depending on play-in games. Seed
       format is a region letter (W/X/Y/Z) followed by a two-digit seed
       number (01-16). Seed is the most predictive single feature in the
       model.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

FILE: MNCAATourneyCompactResults.csv
PURPOSE: Records the outcome of every NCAA tournament game.
COLUMNS: Season, DayNum, WTeamID, WScore, LTeamID, LScore, WLoc, NumOT
NOTES: This is the target variable. W and L prefixes refer to winning
       and losing, not women's. All men's tournament games are played
       at neutral sites so WLoc is always N.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

FILE: MRegularSeasonDetailedResults.csv
PURPOSE: Full box score stats for every regular season game since 2003.
COLUMNS: Season, DayNum, WTeamID, WScore, LTeamID, LScore, WLoc, NumOT,
         plus per-team stats: FGM, FGA, FGM3, FGA3, FTM, FTA, OR, DR,
         Ast, TO, Stl, Blk, PF for both winning and losing teams.
NOTES: Primary feature source. Used to build per-team season averages
       that represent team strength before the tournament starts.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

FILE: MMasseyOrdinals.csv
PURPOSE: Weekly rankings from dozens of external systems including
         KenPom, Sagarin, and RPI going back to 2003.
COLUMNS: Season, RankingDayNum, SystemName, Tea
